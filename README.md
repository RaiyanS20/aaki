# Aaki's Birthday Quest 🎂

A tiny Stardew-style pixel adventure made for Aaki's birthday.

Make your character, then walk through the valley with **Twinkle** the cat. You'll visit
**Whispering Falls**, **Golden Lake** at sunset and the **Sakura Grove**, and find a magical scroll
with a message at each one. At the end there's a surprise in the Starlight Garden: the **Gem Rose**. 🌹💎

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing to install, and it works on phones too.

- **Desktop:** arrow keys / WASD to walk, **Space** to talk, read and pet Twinkle
- **Phone:** drag on the screen to walk, tap **Ⓐ** to interact

To share it as a link: on GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**, pick this branch with folder `/ (root)`, and send her the URL.

## Personalise

Open `js/config.js` to change the names, the three scroll messages, and the Gem Rose note.

Testing tip: `index.html?area=4` jumps straight to the last area (`area=0` to `4`).
